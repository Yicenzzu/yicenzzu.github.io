Research themes spanning atmospheric chemistry, particle-resolved modeling, high-performance computing, and scientific visualization.

    <div><p class="academic-eyebrow">Atmospheric chemistry</p><h2>Aerosol-cloud interactions and atmospheric chemistry</h2><p>Atmospheric aerosols act as cloud condensation nuclei and influence air quality. I study chemical processes between gas and particle phases to better understand the effects of heterogeneous and multiphase reactions on climate and air quality.</p></div>

  <article class="academic-project">

    <div><p class="academic-eyebrow">Particle-resolved modeling</p><h2>Computational simulation of aerosol composition and multiphase chemistry</h2><p>Using particle-resolved models such as <a href="https://github.com/compdyn/partmc">PartMC</a>, I examine how aerosol representation and mixing state affect heterogeneous reactions and model predictions.</p></div>
  </article>
  <article class="academic-project">

    <div><p class="academic-eyebrow">Scientific computing</p><h2>Data science and high-performance computing</h2><p>I develop analysis workflows for large scientific datasets, evaluate model predictions against observations, and run parallel simulations to assess the effects of simplified aerosol representations.</p></div>
  </article>
  <article class="academic-project">

I quantify how these assumptions affect predicted gas and particle concentrations and the interpretation of atmospheric processes.</p></div>
